# DepositPhotos | AI Training Data 

DepositPhotos provides commercially licensed AI training data, including multimodal datasets, for AI/ML development. Explore image, video, audio, text, and 3D datasets enriched with metadata to train machine learning models across diverse applications—including generative AI, computer vision, and speech processing.

## Data Modalities, Formats & AI Applications

* **Generative AI:** Paired text-image, text-video, and prompt-audio datasets with detailed metadata structured for pre-training and fine-tuning GenAI models.
* **Computer Vision:** High-resolution images and 1080p/4K footage (`.jpg`, `.png`, `.mp4`, `.mov`) featuring embedded IPTC/EXIF metadata for object detection, scene tracking, and facial recognition.
* **Speech & NLP:** Conversational audio, call center dialogues, and podcasts (`.wav`, `.flac`) paired with PII-scrubbed transcriptions (`.json`, `.txt`) and speaker attributes for ASR engines, sentiment analysis, and voice synthesis.
* **Spatial Computing & 3D:** Volumetric captures, CGI environments, and 360° HDRIs (`.obj`, `.fbx`, `.exr`) compatible with Unreal Engine 5, Blender, and 3ds Max for robotics, synthetic data generation, and autonomous vehicle simulation.
* **Text & Document Intelligence:** Structured JSON layout templates and document hierarchies for layout synthesis, document parsing, and OCR model training.

## Licensing, Metadata & Privacy

* **Licensing:** Commercially licensed AI-ready data with relevant model and property releases where applicable.
* **Data Preparation:** Embedded metadata and tags designed to minimize data preprocessing.
* **Privacy:** AI training data sourced in compliance with global privacy regulations, including GDPR and CCPA standards.
